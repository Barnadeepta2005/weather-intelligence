# Data Sources & Third-Party Attributions

Atmos is made possible through open data initiatives, public scientific research APIs, and open-source geospatial projects. We gratefully acknowledge the following data sources and contributors:

---

### 1. Open-Meteo
- **Data Provided**: Weather forecasts, hourly atmospheric conditions, 7-day outlooks, UV index, global forward geocoding, and Copernicus CAMS atmospheric dispersion model.
- **Provider**: Open-Meteo GmbH
- **Terms & License**: CC-BY 4.0. Free for non-commercial use with required attribution.
- **URL**: [https://open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com)

---

### 2. Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) / Data.gov.in
- **Data Provided**: Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Station (CAAQMS) real-time observations across major Indian cities (PM2.5, PM10, O3, NO2).
- **Provider**: Central Pollution Control Board, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India.
- **Platform**: Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India (data.gov.in).
- **Terms & License**: Government Open Data License - India (GODL).
- **URL**: [https://data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in)

---

### 3. RainViewer
- **Data Provided**: Global weather radar precipitation tiles and past timestamp metadata.
- **Provider**: RainViewer Inc.
- **Terms & License**: Free API tier for personal, educational, and community non-commercial use.
- **URL**: [https://www.rainviewer.com](https://www.rainviewer.com)

---

### 4. OpenFreeMap & MapLibre
- **Data Provided**: Vector base map tiles (Positron light map style).
- **Render Engine**: MapLibre GL JS (BSD 3-Clause License).
- **Tile Host**: OpenFreeMap ([https://openfreemap.org](https://openfreemap.org)).

---

### 5. OpenStreetMap & OpenMapTiles
- **Data Provided**: Global cartographic geometry and place names.
- **License**: © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors (ODbL) and © [OpenMapTiles](https://openmaptiles.org).

---

### 6. Natural Earth
- **Data Provided**: Admin-0 (Country Boundaries) and Admin-1 (State/Province Boundaries) cultural vector geometry.
- **License**: Public Domain.
- **URL**: [https://www.naturalearthdata.com](https://www.naturalearthdata.com)

---

### 7. Geocoding Services
- **BigDataCloud**: Free reverse-geocoding client endpoint ([https://www.bigdatacloud.com](https://www.bigdatacloud.com)).
- **Nominatim**: OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse geocoder ([https://nominatim.org](https://nominatim.org)).
